Your browser was somewhere else.

Alibi makes websites see the time zone, language and location you pick, so they tell the same story as your VPN.

Get started

  1. 1 Pin Alibi: open the extensions menu (the puzzle piece next to the address bar) and click the pin beside Alibi.
  2. 2 Click the Alibi stamp and choose a cover: one of the places, Match my IP address, or your own time zone, locale and coordinates.
  3. 3 Reload the tab. The postmark at the top of the popup shows what sites now see.

Two modes

Stealth
The default. Rewrites the clock, language and location inside each page and its frames and workers. Chrome shows no banner. Workers loaded from a file still see your real time zone.
Deep
Spoofs below the page, through Chrome’s debugger, including embedded sites and Web Workers, so it’s much harder to detect. Chrome shows a “started debugging this browser” banner while it’s on.

Cover the first moment of every page

Some sites read your time zone the instant a page opens, before Stealth Mode can load. To close that gap:

  1. 1 Open chrome://extensions (in Brave, brave://extensions).
  2. 2 Click Details on Alibi.
  3. 3 Turn on Allow User Scripts. Alibi then runs before any script on the page.

A different cover for each site

The popup has two tabs. All sites is your usual cover. The second tab is named after the site you’re on: choose Give it its own cover and that site, along with its subdomains, gets its own settings.

To keep a site on your real location, such as your bank, give it its own cover and choose Off (real location). Mode applies to all sites.

Your IP address

Alibi doesn’t change your IP address. Use a VPN or proxy for that, then choose Match my IP address so everything agrees. Alibi only contacts GeoJS (get.geojs.io, over HTTPS) when you choose that option.

The debugging banner

Only Deep mode shows it. To keep Deep mode without the banner, start Chrome with the --silent-debugger-extension-api flag (how to run Chrome with flags).
